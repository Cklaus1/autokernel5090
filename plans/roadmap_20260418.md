# Roadmap — 2026-04-18

Anchors this session on a single question: **can consumer-Blackwell inference reach regime-change scale (match H100 single-user at 1/4 silicon cost, or better $/Mtok for batch)?** All tracks feed one of three compounding levers — single-user decode latency, batch aggregate throughput, or context-length / TTFT — and each has a concrete hardware-theoretical ceiling.

Hardware: 2× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q (SM120a), 188 SMs, 228 KB smem/SM, 96 GB GDDR7, 1,792 GB/s HBM peak. WSL2 + AMD 9950X3D host.
